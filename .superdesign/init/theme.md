# MedBridge Global visual foundation

## Hard constraints

- Preserve the current font pairing exactly: Outfit for all interface and editorial copy, Reenie Beanie only for the handwritten emphasis word in the hero.
- Do not introduce another font, a generic healthcare-blue palette, or a visual style that reads as a clinic template.
- Maintain a warm, international-care identity: calm, reliable, discreet, and human.

## Existing tokens

- Canvas: `#FDFCF8`
- Ink: `#292524`
- Muted text: `#78716C`
- Coral accent: `#FFB7B2`
- Sage: `#E8EFE8`
- Lavender: `#EFEDF4`
- Surface: white
- Hairline: `#e5e5e5`
- Core radius: 16px, with large editorial cards currently 24–32px

## Intended redesign direction

Use a restrained clinical-editorial composition: generous whitespace, clear diagnostic hierarchy, subtle coral emphasis, and proof-oriented healthcare signals. Replace the current diffuse pastel treatment with an intentional hero architecture: concise trust metadata, a care-path visual, and calmer section transitions. Use line illustrations, data labels, and structured service cards rather than decorative blobs or app-device mockups. Motion should be quiet (150–250ms) and respect reduced motion.
