# Extractable design components

## Navbar

**Purpose:** Global public-site navigation. Existing source: `src/components/navbar.tsx`.

**Visual contract:** A floating warm-white pill with a coral dot logo, wordmark, four central links, Login link, and dark “Start Match” CTA. The navbar is outside scope for this redesign and must remain visually and behaviorally unchanged.

**Responsive contract:** Navigation links hide below desktop width; Login hides below small width; logo wordmark hides below small width; Start Match remains accessible.

## Hero action pair

**Purpose:** Direct a visitor to treatment discovery or hospital exploration.

**Visual contract:** Primary coral action plus neutral secondary action. Preserve existing contrast and support stacked layout on small screens.

## Journey step card

**Purpose:** Explain a stage in international care coordination.

**Visual contract:** Compact, horizontal-scroll-friendly card with sequence label and short outcome. The redesign may adjust its composition to align with clinical trust, but must retain keyboard and touch access to content.

## Trust marker

**Purpose:** Small informational proof unit for accreditation, privacy, care coordination, or response expectations.

**Visual contract:** Quiet line icon, short label, no decorative gradients or loud badges.
